Six sizes against stack A

Space Grotesk for the loud lines, IBM Plex Sans for reading, IBM Plex Mono for the micro labels. Sizes are fluid where the screen changes width, fixed where they shouldn't.

How to read this page

Resize the window: display and deck scale with the viewport so the poster line keeps its proportion at every width. Body, small, micro and numeral stay fixed — small text that scales with the window stays unreadable on small screens and shouty on large ones.

Stack A holds the tracking at every size: -0.035em tightens display as it grows, +0.11em opens micro so the small caps stay distinguishable. That pair of extremes comes straight from UI-001's measurements on the Voice Lab sheet.
