Three typefaces, picked by looking

The same words are set three times below. Same sizes, same spacing, same colour. The only thing that changes is the shape of the letters. Look at them, then say A, B or C.

Typeface

The design of the letters themselves: the shapes of the a and the g, how thick the strokes are, whether the ends are cut square. One typeface exists in many weights and many sizes.

Font

One file of that design, at one weight. Space Grotesk is a typeface; Space Grotesk Bold is a font. People say "font" for both, which is why the word typeface is being used here.

Role

The job the letters are doing. This page has three: display for the loud line, reading for paragraphs, micro for the small labels. Different jobs want different shapes, so a set is usually three typefaces rather than one.

What to look at. The display line first — does "Three watchers" look like the title of something you would read, or like a logo? Then the paragraph: read two lines and see whether your eye slides. Then the small caps label and the two numbers, because 0x8E18 has a zero and an E in it and every one of these sets draws them differently.

What this does not settle. The sizes are shown smaller than the real page. Once a set is picked, the next step (RD-04) fixes the six sizes, the line heights and the tracking against it — and the display type will get a lot larger, so a face that looks calm here can look loud at poster size.
